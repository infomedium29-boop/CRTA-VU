Privacy Policy

Information about how we handle personal data and your rights.

Last updated: 27 October 2026.

1. Data controller

CRTA VU, construction services business, owner Danijela Hardi, Vijeća Europe 73, 32000 Vukovar, Croatian tax ID (OIB): 81107965589. Contact: crta-vu@gmail.com; phone 032 619 189.

2. What data do we collect?

When you contact us by email or through the contact form, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, type of project and message. We only process the information needed to answer your enquiry and, where relevant, discuss future cooperation.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We use information submitted through enquiries to communicate with you and take steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), or on the basis of our legitimate interest in business communication (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), depending on the enquiry. If optional analytics are enabled in the future, we will request your prior consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

4. Contact forms and email

The contact form currently prepares an email in your email application; no message is sent until you send it yourself. If a form processing service is connected for production use, this notice will be updated as appropriate to identify relevant recipients and processors.

5. Cookies and local storage

The website uses essential local browser storage to remember your cookie preferences. Optional analytics are not enabled without your consent and the necessary configuration. You can change your preferences using the “Cookie settings” link in the footer.

6. Data recipients

Messages may be processed by your email provider and ours. Hosting providers may process basic technical logs required to deliver and secure the website. We do not sell personal information.

7. Data retention

We keep enquiries for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, continue correspondence and potentially work together, or longer if required by law or to protect legal claims. Exact retention periods depend on the business purpose and applicable rules.

8. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your data, as well as data portability and withdrawal of consent without affecting previous lawful processing. To exercise your rights, contact us at the email address above.

9. Complaints to the supervisory authority

If you believe that your personal data is being handled unlawfully, you may lodge a complaint with the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP), azop.hr.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when website functionality, third-party services or legal requirements change. The current version is available on this page.

For any privacy questions, email us at crta-vu@gmail.com.
