Frequently asked questions

Practical answers about starting a project, preparing technical documentation and planning construction work.

Services and working together

What types of project documentation do you prepare?

The scope of documentation depends on the project. We consider the building type, available information and the purpose of the proposed work.

When should I get in touch about my idea?

Ideally at the very beginning, while options are still open and before major investment decisions have been made.

Can you work on older, existing buildings?

Existing buildings often require additional information and measured surveys before the next steps can be planned.

Do you carry out existing condition surveys?

Architectural surveys of existing buildings are among the activities documented in CRTA VU’s publicly available project references.

What should I include in my first enquiry?

Send a brief description of the proposed work, its location and your goal. Include existing drawings, photographs or other documents if you have them.

How long does it take to prepare project documentation?

The timeframe depends on the complexity, available information, required documentation and any relevant administrative procedures.

Can I request a bill of quantities?

Bills of quantities may form part of project preparation. The content and scope are agreed individually for each assignment.

Do you also carry out construction work?

CRTA VU is presented here as a business specialising in project and technical documentation. Construction work itself is not covered by this website.
